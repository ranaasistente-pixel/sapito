Sapito Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sapito may collect and how that information is used to operate the game, protect purchases, and support players.

Back to Sapito Terms of Service

1. Information We May Collect

  • Discord account identifiers, such as Discord user ID, when you use Sapito through Discord.
  • Game progress, including level, rewards, food balance, Sapito Coin balance, owned items, skins, tattoos, and puzzle progress.
  • Purchase and entitlement records, including product pack, transaction ID, purchase status, and coin credits.
  • Support information you choose to send, such as bug reports, malfunction reports, or purchase support requests.
  • Basic technical information, such as server logs, error logs, device/browser type, timestamps, and approximate usage events needed to keep the app working.
  • Local browser storage may be used for non-sensitive game settings and local progress during testing or offline/local play.

2. Information We Do Not Want

Sapito does not ask you to send bank details, tax information, passwords, Discord backup codes, or payment card numbers. Real-money payments should be handled by Discord or an approved payment platform.

3. How We Use Information

  • To run Sapito and save your game progress.
  • To display and protect Sapito Coin balances.
  • To verify purchases, prevent duplicate credits, and investigate missing purchases.
  • To provide support and fix bugs or malfunctions.
  • To detect abuse, cheating, fraud, or attempts to fake coin credits.
  • To comply with legal, tax, payment, platform, or Discord policy requirements.

4. Sharing Information

We may share limited information with Discord, payment providers, hosting providers, database providers, support tools, or legal/compliance services when needed to operate Sapito, verify purchases, prevent abuse, or comply with law and platform rules.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Payments

Sapito does not directly collect payment card numbers. Purchases should be processed by Discord or an approved payment platform. Sapito may receive purchase confirmation, entitlement, SKU, transaction, and status information so coins can be credited correctly.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Sapito may keep game progress, coin ledger records, and purchase records for as long as needed to operate the app, support users, prevent fraud, comply with platform rules, or meet legal obligations. Some records may be kept after account deletion requests if required for fraud prevention, payment disputes, accounting, or legal compliance.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect Sapito data. No system can be guaranteed 100 percent secure. You are responsible for protecting your Discord account and devices.

8. Children

Sapito is not directed to children under the minimum age required by Discord or applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information improperly, contact support so it can be reviewed.

9. Your Choices

You may stop using Sapito at any time. You may also contact support to ask about access, correction, deletion, or privacy questions. Some requests may require identity verification and may be limited by Discord rules, payment records, fraud prevention, or legal requirements.

10. International Use

Sapito may be hosted or processed in locations different from where you live. By using Sapito, you understand that information may be processed where the app, hosting provider, Discord, or payment provider operates.

11. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, purchase support, or malfunction reports, contact the creator through the official Sapito support channel in Discord or the contact method listed on Sapito's Discord app page.
